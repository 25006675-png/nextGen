01 — The problem

Waste isn't the end. Landfill is.

Over-ordered stock, prep scraps, plate leftovers — it all goes in one bin, and that bin goes to landfill. We make it the start of a loop instead.

40 t

of food waste leaves Taylor's Lakeside every month. All of it goes to landfill.

25%

of it is avoidable: food that was bought or cooked but never needed.

RM 4,800

paid to waste haulers every month just to cart it away.

02 — How the loop works

Four steps. Nothing leaves the loop.

01

Track

Log what each food court buys and weigh what comes back as waste.

02

Collect

Sealed bins in every food court, picked up daily, kept apart from general rubbish.

03

Digest

Black soldier fly larvae eat through the waste and grow into protein.

04

Return

Larvae become farm feed. What is left undigested becomes frass fertiliser.

04 — What the waste becomes

One bin in. Two products out.

Larvae → protein feed

Grown larvae are supplied to poultry and livestock farms as a local protein feed.

For: poultry & livestock farms

Frass → fertiliser

What the larvae leave behind is cured into an organic soil amendment for growers.

For: Taylor's campus landscaping and local growers
