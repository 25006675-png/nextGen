01 — The problem

Waste isn't the end. Landfill is.

Each Malaysian wastes 81 kg of food a year, while food-aid requests rose by up to 35–40%. Prep scraps, plate leftovers and unsold food all go in one bin, and the bin goes to landfill.

02 — Insights

Three parties are paying for the same problem.

F&B tenants

Pay to dispose of food waste, with no long-term solution.

BSF programme

Taylor's partnership with Entomal struggles with unpredictable waste volumes.

Taylor's University

Its sustainability disclosure has yet to report food-service waste.

Key takeaway: there is already a way to turn food waste into value, but the pipeline is broken.

05 — Impact & feasibility

One year at Taylor's. Two products out.

292 t

of kitchen waste diverted every year

350 t

Feasibility

Builds on existing infrastructure

Plugs into Taylor's current BSF programme with Entomal.

Ultra-lean capex

Runs on a simple waste log for tenants, not new machinery.

No compliance concerns

Works within the campus's current waste-handling setup.

Larvae → insect protein

Grown larvae become a local protein feed for poultry and livestock farms.

For: poultry & livestock farms

Frass → bio-fertiliser

What the larvae leave behind becomes a natural, chitin-rich bio-fertiliser.

For: Taylor's campus landscaping and local growers
